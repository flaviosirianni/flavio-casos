Staffing services and HR consulting

Corporate websites and job portal

Websites with a job portal and API

Bilingual websites with job search, résumé submission and forms connected to the team.

Technical implementation

I implemented Astro interfaces with React components and Hono APIs on Cloudflare Workers. Adapters separate jobs and applications in Google Sheets, résumés in R2 and notifications through Resend.

What it solves

Companies and candidates need distinct journeys on the same website. Structured forms collect the details needed to follow up on enquiries and applications.

Flow and scope

The candidate filters openings, reads the details and attaches a résumé. The API validates the submission, stores the file and records the application. The interface offers Spanish and English content.

  • Job listings with filters and pagination.
  • Form and attachment validation.
  • Application recording and team notifications.
