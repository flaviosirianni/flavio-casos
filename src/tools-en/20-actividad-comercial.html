Daily sales activity tracking

Sales rules and an explainable daily archive · Implemented

Evaluate daily activity by agent, store the reason for each result and retain history with controlled corrections.

Technical implementation

Apps Script imports a BI CSV and evaluates conversations, calls, emails, messages and open houses. The archive uses an agent and operational date key.

Problem solved

Shows why a daily goal was or was not met without relying on an isolated figure that later changes.

Flow and scope

An open house qualifies directly. Otherwise, one conversation or five outbound activities is required, together with an empty cleanup list. The current day is replaced; yesterday allows upward email and text corrections.

  • Calls + emails + texts make up outbound activity.
  • The result and reasons are stored alongside evaluation values.
  • Operational date calculated in America/Phoenix.
