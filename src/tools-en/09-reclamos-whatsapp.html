Service issue management through WhatsApp

Issue-tracking bot

The reporter chooses a location, describes the problem and attaches evidence. The coordinator receives the case, records the solution and requests the reporter’s acceptance.

Technical implementation

I developed a FastAPI webhook for WhatsApp Cloud API, with conversation state in Firestore, records in Sheets and files in Drive. The flow checks roles, deduplicates messages and protects state transitions.

What it solves

It centralizes issues that could otherwise remain scattered across conversations without traceability. It retains location, evidence, responsibility and timing for follow-up and analysis.

Flow and scope

The case moves from creation to receipt, resolution and acceptance. Rejection opens follow-up; reminders and alerts cover pending items. Analytical outputs are prepared in BigQuery.

  • Location by building, floor and area, with free-text details.
  • Initial and resolution evidence with file links.
  • Atomic transitions, deduplication and webhook validation.
