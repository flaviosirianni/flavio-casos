Institutional food services · multiple sites

Consolidating service billing data

Service-quantity consolidation

Breakfast, lunch, afternoon meal and dinner quantities are combined from separate sheets by site and diner type. The master table becomes available to the analytical layer.

Technical implementation

I implemented an Apps Script that identifies source sheets, normalizes dates and quantities and consolidates a master union. It logs runs and checks that no row has lost its date before writing the output.

What it solves

It avoids manually copying activity from each site and category. It prepares comparable quantities for billing reports and staffing comparisons; it does not issue tax invoices.

Flow and scope

Sources are first refreshed by site, then the master table is rebuilt from those local copies. These are separate operations: rebuilding the union does not reread source files.

  • Diner types: patients, companions and authorized diners.
  • Integer or blank quantities, with issue logging.
  • The union aborts without writing if it detects rows without dates.

Data flow

Source
Sheets by site and category
Processing
Normalization and master table
Output
BigQuery and reports

The master table is the tabular source read by BigQuery. Updates depend on source refreshes followed by consolidation.
