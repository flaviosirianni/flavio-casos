Stock control across multiple sites

Stock counting and reporting web app

The team counts supplies on a phone and views the latest value alongside the previous count. Pack sizes are grouped by raw material to compare useful quantities.

Technical implementation

I developed a Next.js web app with Supabase Auth, role and plant permissions, and PostgreSQL storage. Current stock is accompanied by history; BigQuery and Superset outputs support trend analysis.

What it solves

It replaces scattered counts and comparisons between different pack sizes. It shows how much stock exists, where it is and when it was counted.

Flow and scope

Users choose an authorized plant, record quantities by product and view stock and history. Tags and pack contents allow raw-material aggregation; minimum levels add replenishment references.

  • Roles and plant access are also enforced in the database.
  • AI tag suggestions with a manual fallback.
  • Exports and an embedded dashboard with access filters.

Data flow

Source
Plant stock count
Processing
Supabase and history
Output
Stock and dashboard

Operational entries are stored in Supabase; synchronization prepares the data queried by BigQuery and the dashboard.
