Personal product · family health

Health information with context

Mobile app and Telegram assistant

Profiles, documents and follow-ups that help prepare appointments and preserve each person’s context.

Technical implementation

I built an Expo mobile app with Node.js/Express services and a Telegram bot. SQLite stores profiles, documents, episodes and follow-ups; OpenAI processes the conversation context.

What it solves

Family health information is often scattered across files and messages. The system organizes it by person and retrieves context to prepare a professional appointment.

Flow and scope

The flow starts with a profile, adds documents and conversations, and supports reviewing extracted information and follow-ups. It provides organization and information; medical decisions belong to the professional.

  • Separate profiles for different people.
  • Documents, episodes and consultations linked to a profile.
  • Reviewable extracted information and appointment questions.
