Personal product · wellbeing

Conversational food logging

Telegram bot with catalog and persistent summaries · Implemented

Turn meal and weigh-in messages into reviewable records, distinguish estimates and query totals from stored data.

Technical implementation

The bot resolves quantities, units and dates in the user’s time zone. It combines catalog, preferences and OpenAI interpretation; intakes and weigh-ins are stored in SQLite with operation receipts.

Problem solved

Makes everyday logging possible without filling in a spreadsheet while retaining the source of nutritional values.

Flow and scope

Includes a catalog, label reading, record corrections, weigh-ins and daily summaries. Totals are calculated from persisted data by person and date.

  • Labels estimated values and catalog or label sources.
  • Supports record review and temporal corrections.
  • Meals, profile and weigh-ins are kept separate.
