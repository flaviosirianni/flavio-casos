Purchasing and inventory planning

Inventory analytics model under validation

Monthly model that projects inventory and calculates purchasing capacity by category and location.

Technical implementation

I implemented SQL queries that normalize budget, inventory, order and sales reports to department, class, subclass, location and month. Python scripts support dataset ingestion and review.

What it solves

Deciding how much to buy requires anticipating closing inventory and comparing it with budget. The model makes inputs and the purchasing-capacity formula explicit.

Flow and scope

The calculation combines opening inventory, open orders and receipts, less sales and markdowns. It uses actuals for closed months and budgets to project open months. Integration and data quality remain under validation.

  • Grain and types normalized before joining reports.
  • Monthly capacity and cumulative capacity from the current month.
  • Arithmetic demo using fictional amounts.
