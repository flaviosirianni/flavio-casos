Payroll hours preparation and review

Payroll calculation and archive web app · Implemented

Choose a period and employees, review hours and warnings, and save a run with its source dates and payment status.

Technical implementation

Server-side calculations use snapshots of time entries, PTO and holidays. Rules vary by employee type and consult a ledger of overtime already included in previous runs.

Problem solved

Brings separate sources together and shows how payable hours are calculated before a run is posted.

Flow and scope

The preview supports filtering and CSV export. Posting stores lines and source dates; saved runs can be reviewed and marked paid without recalculation.

  • Overtime is calculated only for closed weeks.
  • PTO and holidays are kept separate from worked time for overtime.
  • Warnings flag incomplete data and overlapping work and PTO.

Data flow

Time entries + PTO + holidays
↓
Calculation and review
↓
Saved run + overtime ledger
