Weekly compensation review

Implemented compensation model under validation

Employee-by-week model combining hours, commissions and rates to review differences.

Technical implementation

I built an employee-by-week fact table with source normalization and deduplication, weekly allocation of monthly commissions and historical rates. A worksheet records business decisions awaiting validation.

What it solves

Weekly review requires combining sources with different grains. The model makes it possible to inspect the hours, commission and rate behind a result.

Flow and scope

Closed-month commissions are allocated by calendar day. A week crossing two months waits until both are closed. The calculation separates base pay, regular rate and overtime premium; rules remain under validation.

  • Sources preserved in mirror worksheets.
  • Rates resolved by effective date.
  • Rules and decisions log; no guarantee of legal compliance.
