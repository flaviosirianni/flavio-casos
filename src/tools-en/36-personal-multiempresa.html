Facility services · daily attendance evolution

Personnel management across organizations

Multi-organization web app prototype

Web prototype with employees, shifts, absences and attendance separated by organization.

Technical implementation

I implemented domain modules in Next.js and a Prisma schema on PostgreSQL. Employees, catalogues, absences, attendance and audit records include the organization; services apply entry, posting and confirmation rules.

What it solves

Daily attendance needs consistency when several organizations share an application. The prototype models each organization’s data and settings separately.

Flow and scope

The user chooses an organization and date, records statuses and hours, posts rows and confirms the day once rules are satisfied. It includes services for absences, historical queries and monthly attendance. Its status remains a prototype.

  • Organization-specific settings and catalogues.
  • Absence rules and time validation.
  • Change auditing and transactional confirmation.
