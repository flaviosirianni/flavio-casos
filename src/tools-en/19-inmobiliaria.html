Back office connected to Google Sheets with quality checks · Implemented

Bring accounts, contacts, users, transactions and commissions into shared tables, with incremental synchronization and possible duplicate reports.

Technical implementation

The REST client paginates results, respects limits and retains per-resource cursors. Rows are updated by ID; transactions are enriched with accounts and commission totals.

Problem solved

Provides a queryable base outside the back office and surfaces contact issues before manual cleanup.

Flow and scope

The initial load can resume from its cursor; subsequent runs retrieve changes. Contact checks report exact and fuzzy matches for human review.

  • State, last run and errors per resource.
  • New fields added to the spreadsheet schema.
  • Quality reports; contacts are not merged automatically.
