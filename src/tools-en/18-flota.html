Fleet route and activity analysis

Telemetry converted into daily summaries · Implemented

Turn GPS segments into one row per driver and day, with times, stops, distance and office visits.

Technical implementation

The pipeline retrieves drivers and segments by UTC window, retains a source fingerprint and rebuilds the daily summary. Geofences help identify office visits.

Problem solved

Makes a workday review possible without manually interpreting every vehicle’s GPS segments.

Flow and scope

Calculates workday anchors, distinct destinations and total distance. Rebuilding replaces the selected day and removes duplicates; times are shown in UTC.

  • Filters tiny movements when detecting workday anchors.
  • Retains segment count and source fingerprint.
  • Supports range reprocessing and summary-only rebuilding.
