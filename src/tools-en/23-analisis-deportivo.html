Sports research and tracking assistant

Telegram bot with research and personal tracking · Implemented

Choose an event and matchup, consult statistics and recent signals, and keep a personal ledger with reviewable operations.

Technical implementation

Node.js coordinates statistics, odds and web information tools with OpenAI and SQLite. A gate validates event identity, status and freshness before allowing consumption.

Problem solved

Reduces confusion between cards and matchups and separates research from persistent operation records.

Flow and scope

The current configuration prioritizes guided menus and session states. The personal ledger provides previews and confirmations for sensitive operations; results are not settled automatically without sufficient evidence.

  • Checks dates, sources and verification expiry.
  • History and odds treated as research signals.
  • SQLite persistence and change auditing.
