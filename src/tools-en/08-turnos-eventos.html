Shift coordination between client and provider

Operational planning and negotiation web app

Client and provider work on the same week: events, areas and schedules. Changes pass through requests and responses until an agreed version is reached.

Technical implementation

I implemented an Apps Script web app with role-based views and Sheets storage. Services separate weeks, assignments, requests, auditing, notifications, published versions and PDF generation.

What it solves

It avoids discussing schedules across different copies of a spreadsheet. Each request retains the affected assignment, reason and response, showing what was proposed and what was applied.

Flow and scope

The week is prepared and published, changes are negotiated and accepted decisions are applied. Closing checks pending items and agreement; versions and the PDF preserve the agreed output.

  • Requests for time changes, replacements, areas and additional staff.
  • Duplicate checks by person and day.
  • Action history, comments and final agreement.
