Team distributed across countries

Time off tracking and reminders

Approved PTO web app with daily BI output · Implemented

Record already approved time off, count applicable days and notify the team through Slack.

Technical implementation

Google Apps Script validates ranges, country and overlaps. It expands active requests and calendar history into daily rows, excluding weekends and country holidays.

Problem solved

Avoids counting an absence twice or applying another country’s holiday calendar.

Flow and scope

Includes creation, editing, cancellation and departure and return reminders. Slack failures are logged without blocking PTO storage.

  • Entries represent already approved PTO; there is no managerial approval workflow.
  • Ranges with no counted days and overlapping dates are rejected.
  • One row per person and active day for BI ingestion.
