Consolidating attendance from separate spreadsheets

Operational spreadsheet normalization

Each site can keep its working grid. A master process reads the months, interprets dates and statuses and generates comparable rows for analysis.

Technical implementation

I built a modular Apps Script flow to detect monthly sheets, infer their layout and transform cells into records. It includes incremental updates, historical reimports and configurable deduplication by person, date and shift.

What it solves

It reduces the work of copying heterogeneous grids into a central table. Codes are matched with a status catalog so sites share an output structure.

Flow and scope

The normal run rereads a recent window and preserves earlier history. Full rebuilds are also available; source failures are logged and must not be interpreted as a complete load.

  • Detection of month, year, dates and shift markers.
  • One row per recorded status, with a normalized description.
  • Global lock, retries and per-source diagnostics.
